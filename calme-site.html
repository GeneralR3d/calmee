The skin barrier

Healthy skin holds on to water.

The top layer of skin is a seal of flattened cells. Beneath it, water and lipids keep skin cushioned, supple and calm.

Eczema-prone skin

When the barrier cracks, moisture escapes.

Gaps open between skin cells. Water drains away and irritants like sweat and fragrance get in, so skin turns tight, hot and itchy.

What Calme does

Calme helps seal it back.

Ceramides, cholesterol and fatty acids in a 3:1:1 ratio, the same ratio healthy skin uses, fill the gaps so skin can hold water again.

Questions? Answers.

Will Calme treat my eczema?

No skincare can promise that. Calme supports a healthy skin barrier and comforts dryness. Keep using anything your doctor prescribes, and see a doctor for flare-ups that don't settle.

Can I use acids and retinol on eczema-prone skin?

Only when skin is calm. Use PHA Gentle Exfoliant and Night Renew Retinol on two evenings a week each, never on the same night and never during a flare. Patch test first.
